LUNHS v34 — ARCHIVE & BACKUP

This update adds:
- CSV/Excel-compatible export of student enrollment
- grade backup
- teacher-load backup
- section/adviser backup
- school-year archive status
- protection against archiving the currently ACTIVE school year
- no passwords/authentication secrets in exports

INSTALL:
1. Run V34-ARCHIVE-BACKUP.sql ONCE in Supabase SQL Editor.
2. Upload admin-archive.html to the root of LUNHS-PORTAL.
3. Add the card in ADMIN-LINK-SNIPPET.txt to admin.html.
4. Refresh/reopen the portal.

IMPORTANT:
Archiving does NOT delete records. Before archiving, export the records and keep copies in a secure school-controlled location.
