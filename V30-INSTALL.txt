LUNHS PORTAL v30 — MPS GRADE ANALYTICS

ADMIN DASHBOARD now includes MPS Grade Analytics.

REPORTS:
1. MPS per Subject and Grade Level
   - Subject
   - Grade Level
   - Number of students
   - Term 1 MPS
   - Term 2 MPS
   - Term 3 MPS
   - Overall MPS

2. Overall MPS per Subject
   - Combines all grade levels for each subject
   - Term 1, Term 2, Term 3 and Overall MPS

3. Overall School MPS summary.

FORMULA USED:
Term MPS = arithmetic mean of all encoded student grades for that group.
Overall MPS = mean of the available Term 1, Term 2 and Term 3 MPS values.

Also includes School Year filter and Print button.

INSTALL:
Upload/replace all files from this ZIP in LUNHS-PORTAL.
No new SQL is required if v29 Three-Term Grading SQL was already run.

NOTE:
This report treats the encoded percentage grades as the percentage scores used for MPS.
