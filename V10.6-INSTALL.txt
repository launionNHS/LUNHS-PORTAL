LUNHS PORTAL v10.6 — DUAL PASSWORD MANAGEMENT

PASSWORD OPTIONS
1. ADMINISTRATOR
- Can reset Student/Teacher passwords in Admin > Users.
- Can change own password while signed in.
- If locked out, can use Administrator email recovery.

2. STUDENT / TEACHER
- Can change their OWN password after signing in.
- Must enter their CURRENT password first.
- Cannot change another user's password.
- No email account is required.
