LUNHS PORTAL v29 — 3 TERM GRADING

Student Dashboard:
Term 1 | Term 2 | Term 3 | Final Average

Teacher Gradebook:
Term 1 | Term 2 | Term 3

Final Average = (Term 1 + Term 2 + Term 3) / 3 when all three terms are present.

INSTALL:
1. Upload/replace all files in this ZIP to LUNHS-PORTAL.
2. In Supabase SQL Editor, run V29-THREE-TERM-GRADING.sql once.
3. Refresh the site with Ctrl+F5.

The migration keeps the old q1-q4 columns for safety and copies existing Q1-Q3 values to the new Term 1-Term 3 columns.
