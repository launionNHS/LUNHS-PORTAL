RECOVER ADMIN
1. Open LUNHS Portal.
2. In the login username box, enter the ORIGINAL Administrator email.
3. Click “Forgot Administrator password?”
4. Open the NEWEST recovery email.
5. Click the recovery link.
6. It should open:
   https://lolitodeloso-afk.github.io/lunhs/reset-password.html
7. Enter and confirm a new password (8+ characters).
8. Return to the portal.
9. Login with the original Admin email + new password.
